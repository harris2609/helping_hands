How recovery works here

மீட்சிக்கான பாதை

Recovery is not one big step. It is a series of small ones, and we walk each of them with you.

  1. You call us

    Talk to our team in Tamil or English. Tell us what is happening at home. We explain how admission works and what it costs.

  2. Assessment

    We understand the person's drinking or drug use, their health and their family situation, and plan treatment around them.

  3. Safe detox

    Stopping suddenly can be dangerous. Withdrawal is managed carefully, under medical supervision, until the body is steady.

  4. Counselling and therapy

    One-to-one and group sessions to understand what drives the habit, rebuild a daily routine and learn how to say no.

  5. Family sessions

    Addiction hurts the whole home. We help families heal too, and prepare everyone for the day the person returns.

  6. Aftercare

    Recovery continues after discharge. We stay in touch with follow-ups, and you always have a number to call when it gets hard.

For families

குடும்பத்தினருக்கு

Most calls we get are from a wife, a mother or a son. If you are worried about someone, this is for you.

Signs that someone needs help

  • Drinks or uses every day, or cannot stop once they start
  • Hides bottles, lies about money or misses work
  • Shakes, sweats or cannot sleep without a drink
  • Anger, fights or violence at home
  • Has promised to stop before, and could not

How to talk to them

  • Pick a calm moment, when they are sober
  • Talk about what you have seen, not about who they are
  • Avoid shouting, shaming or threats; they push people away
  • Offer one small step: “Let us just call and ask.”

Please don't force a heavy drinker to stop suddenly at home

Alcohol withdrawal can cause fits and confusion, and can be life-threatening. Call us first and we will tell you what is safe.

Questions families ask

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள்

Is Healing Hands government approved?

Yes. Healing Hands Foundation is a government-approved rehabilitation centre, registration number 4/93/2026.

Will anyone find out?

No. What you tell us stays between you, your family and our team. We do not share anyone's details with employers, neighbours or relatives.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on the person, the substance and how long it has been used. After the first assessment we tell you the recommended length of stay before admission.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the treatment plan. Call us and we will explain the cost clearly on the first call, before anything is decided.

He refuses to come. What can we do?

Treatment works best when the person agrees to it. Call us first. We will help you plan how to talk to them, and what to do if they say no.

Can the family visit?

Yes. Families are part of recovery and take part in family sessions. Ask us about visiting days when you call.
